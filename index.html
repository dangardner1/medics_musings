The Chart Note

What is Medics Musings?

Medics Musings is a podcast of AI-generated conversations created by physicians Leo A. Gordon, MD, and Dan Gardner, MD, taking on healthcare and beyond with quirky wit, clinical perspective, just enough evidence, plenty of AI, and unfiltered humor.

From medicine's everyday absurdities and healthcare's latest plot twists to artificial intelligence, technology, culture, and whatever else wanders into the exam room, Leo and Dan deliver sharp, irreverent commentary with a physician's eye and a comedian's timing.

On Rounds

Your hosts

Leo A. Gordon, MD

General Surgeon

Dr. Gordon is a seasoned general surgeon with a sharp scalpel and an even sharper wit. He's spent years in the operating room, but it's his ability to weave humor into the most serious of situations that makes him a master storyteller. Whether he's recounting bizarre medical cases or sharing hilarious moments from his career, Leo's stories will have you laughing out loud while learning something new. No one knows with certainty who said "the world belongs to the storytellers" — but they certainly had Dr. Gordon in mind.

Contact Dr. Gordon for humorous educational speeches: leogordonmd@gmail.com.

Dan Gardner, MD

Psychiatrist & Psychoanalyst

A psychiatrist and psychoanalyst, Dr. Gardner brings his optimism and good humor from the consulting room to share relatable, engaging perspectives on his career and medical experience. His unique blend of traditional psychiatric expertise and tech interest means he's equally comfortable discussing your unconscious mind or teaching an AI chatbot about comedic timing.
