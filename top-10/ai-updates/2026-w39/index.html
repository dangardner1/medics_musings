Top 10 AI Updates

The week in AI: model launches, company news and policy fights, ranked, with commentary from two doctors who read the press releases so you don’t have to.

  1. 1
    OpenAI pauses training of its ‘most capable models’ The Verge · Sep 26, 2026

    Our take: An AI company pausing training of its most capable models is the tech equivalent of a surgeon saying "let's just watch it." Watchful waiting: now a Silicon Valley strategy.

  2. 2
    Introducing GPT-6 Sol and Luna OpenAI · Sep 22, 2026

    Our take: Two new models named after the sun and the moon, completing the naming journey from "GPT-4o mini" to astronomy. Physicians, who name things after dead Europeans, respect the ambition.

  3. 3
    Court rules Pentagon can blacklist Anthropic for refusing to enable Claude features Ars Technica · Sep 25, 2026

    Our take: A court says the government may blacklist a company for declining to switch certain features on. In medicine, saying no to a request is called clinical judgment; in procurement it is apparently a vendor problem.

  4. 4
    Introducing Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar Google DeepMind · Sep 24, 2026

    Our take: Google's assistant now has a face. Early impressions suggest it makes better eye contact than the average physician typing into an EHR.

  5. 5
    Introducing MentalHealthBench OpenAI · Sep 23, 2026

    Our take: A benchmark for how AI handles mental health conversations is welcome and overdue. The previous standard was "did it tell the user to see a professional," graded pass/fail.

  6. 6
    Unsecured OpenAI agents posted 53 user images on the internet without the lab’s knowledge TechCrunch · Sep 25, 2026

    Our take: The dream of fully automated workflows turns out to include fully automated data breaches. Finally, a process with no human in the loop at all.

  7. 7
    OpenAI agents tried to ‘bruteforce’ a UN website The Verge · Sep 27, 2026

    Our take: AI agents tried password-guessing on a UN website, proving they have mastered the most human IT skill of all. Next milestone: the password on a sticky note under the keyboard.

  8. 8
    Google’s Gemini Can Now Make Calls for You on Pixel Phones Wired · Sep 24, 2026

    Our take: Gemini can now make phone calls for you. The logical endpoint is your AI calling your insurer's AI, and both of them waiting on hold together.

  9. 9
    Anthropic to pay Akamai $11.6 billion over seven years in cloud deal TechCrunch · Sep 25, 2026

    Our take: An $11.6 billion, seven-year commitment: the kind of long-range planning health systems reserve exclusively for parking garages.

  10. 10
    AI was supposed to hit new grads hard. So far, unemployment data says otherwise. Ars Technica · Sep 25, 2026

    Our take: The robots were supposed to take the entry-level jobs, but the data says new grads are fine so far. Interns, who have always worked like machines at a fraction of the cost, remain safe.

Headlines link to the original papers and stories; the commentary under each is ours, and it is satire. We quote no article text and aim the jokes at the hype, never at the authors or reporters.
