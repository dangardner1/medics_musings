Explained

Medical terms, explained

Plain-English explainers for the medical terms our episodes keep poking fun at. The jokes are ours; the facts are checked. Each one links to the episodes where the topic comes up.

  • Prior authorization What is prior authorization? The paperwork your insurer wants filed before it pays for the care your doctor already said you need. It's the reason this show's tagline is “no prior authorization…
  • AI scribes AI scribes, explained Your doctor used to type while you talked. Now an app listens and types for them. What could possibly get lost in translation?
  • AI chatbots and medical advice Can you trust an AI chatbot with medical questions? Our resident chatbot, Algo Chats-a-Lot III, recommends two bytes and a call in the morning. Real chatbots are more useful than that, and occasionally just as confident.
  • Inguinal hernia What is an inguinal hernia? The most contested territory in the groin, and the subject of our three-part peace summit, Pax Inguinalis.
  • Laparoscopic surgery What is laparoscopic surgery? Operating through keyholes with chopsticks while watching TV. Dr. Leo Gordon credits ten famous physicists for the difficulties, none of whom agreed to it.
  • Robotic surgery How does robotic surgery work? The robot doesn't operate on you. A surgeon does, from across the room, in what Dr. Gordon calls a high-tech romance gone digital.
  • Gallstones Gallstones and gallbladder surgery, explained The official sport of Fourth and Gall and the livelihood of the International Brotherhood of Biliary Tract Workers.
  • Physician burnout What is physician burnout? Freud's cure, in our entirely fictional telling, was stand-up comedy. The real causes are less funny and more fixable.
  • Transference What is transference in therapy? We called a therapy session like a football game. Transference is the play everyone's watching for.
  • Caloric test What is the caloric test (ice water in the ear)? In The Iceman Cometh, a 1972 medical student is handed a bucket of ice water and told to find a brain tumor with it. Here's what the test actually does.
  • Grand rounds What are grand rounds? Weekly hospital lectures where the coffee is lukewarm and the questions are hot. Dr. Gordon has ten commandments for surviving them.
  • Care robots Robots in elder care: what they do and don't do Gerald has a 4.8-star rating and asks permission before every step. He still can't replace your Sunday visit.
