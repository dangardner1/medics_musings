# Outreach kit: directories, medical outlets and communities

Backlinks and listings that send listeners to medicsmusings.com. None of this can
be automated: each one needs you (or Leo) signed in to your own account. Work
through it roughly in order; the first block takes an afternoon.

Links to use everywhere:

- Site: https://www.medicsmusings.com
- Podcast RSS: https://anchor.fm/s/117844514/podcast/rss
- Apple Podcasts: https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/medics-musings/id1780716650
- Short episode links: `medicsmusings.com/e/<n>` (shown under "Embed this episode" on each episode page)

## 1. Podcast directories (claim and complete the listing)

| Where | What to do | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Podchaser (podchaser.com) | Search for the show, "Claim podcast" (verification email goes to the RSS feed's owner address), add both hosts as creators with photos and bios, link the website | Podchaser credits feed into many apps and guest-booking searches |
| Listen Notes (listennotes.com) | Find the show, claim it, fill in website and socials | Its search API powers many smaller apps; a claimed listing ranks better |
| Podcast Index (podcastindex.org) | Check the show is listed; add it by RSS if not | Feeds Fountain, Podverse, Podcast Guru and other "Podcasting 2.0" apps |
| Goodpods (goodpods.com) | Claim the show | Social podcast app with charts that surface small shows |
| Castbox, Podcast Addict, Player FM | Search; submit the RSS if missing | Free installs of your audience on Android |
| iHeartRadio, Audible, Deezer, Pandora | Submit the RSS through each one's podcaster portal (Spotify for Creators also offers one-click distribution to some) | Reach listeners who never open Apple or Spotify |
| YouTube | Make sure full episodes are in a Podcast playlist (YouTube Studio → Content → Playlists → set as podcast) | YouTube is now the largest podcast platform; podcast playlists appear in YouTube Music |

On every listing, the website field should be https://www.medicsmusings.com.

## 2. Medical outlets (guest posts with a link back)

Each piece should be a real, standalone essay, not an ad. End with a one-line bio
linking to the show or a specific episode page.

- **KevinMD** (kevinmd.com): accepts essays from physicians through its
  submission page. Good fits: "What prior authorization taught me about satire"
  (link /explained/prior-authorization/), or Dr. Gordon's Out of Touch essay,
  adapted, linking /episodes/out-of-touch/.
- **Doximity Op-Med** (opmed.doximity.com): physician-written opinion pieces,
  read widely by clinicians. Good fit: "Why I let a robot narrate my patients'
  dignity" (link /episodes/the-robot-who-wipes-my-butt/).
- **Medical humanities journals and blogs** (Pulse, Hektoen International, and
  the "A Piece of My Mind"-style columns in specialty journals): Dr. Gordon's
  essays are a natural fit; mention the podcast version in the bio.
- **Specialty society newsletters** (surgery, geriatrics, psychiatry): offer an
  episode plus its teaching guide (/teach/...) as a "for your next didactic"
  item. Programs link to resources they use.
- **Residency program directors and chief residents**: send the /teach/ page
  with a two-line note. Program sites that list it are excellent backlinks.

## 3. Communities (participate first, post second)

Most medical subreddits remove self-promotion. Read each one's rules, comment
helpfully for a while, then share where it's allowed.

- **r/medicine**: verified-clinician community with strict rules; share only if
  a thread invites it (e.g. humor or prior authorization threads), linking the
  relevant explainer rather than the podcast.
- **r/Residency, r/medicalschool**: the teaching guides and grand rounds
  episode are the angle ("free discussion guide for your next didactic").
- **r/surgery, r/GeneralSurgery**: Fourth and Gall, the Presidential Address and
  Pax Inguinalis.
- **r/podcasts, r/podcast**: weekly self-promotion threads.
- **LinkedIn**: post a quote card (Share a quote → LinkedIn on any episode page)
  once or twice a week, tagging the topic. Doctors are there.

Pre-written blurb (edit freely):

> Medics Musings is a medical satire podcast by two physicians, Leo A. Gordon, MD
> (general surgery) and Dan Gardner, MD. Short episodes on prior authorization,
> AI in the exam room, hernia diplomacy and the absurdities of hospital life.
> Free teaching guides for residency programs at medicsmusings.com/teach.

## 4. Keep it going

- Send listeners who submit a story (via /submit/) the episode link when it airs
  and ask them to share it; that's the loop the submission page is for.
- Check Google Search Console monthly: Performance → Pages, to see which
  explainers and episodes are picking up searches, and write the next explainer
  about whatever people are searching for.
- Track where listeners come from in GA4: Acquisition → Traffic acquisition,
  filtered by `utm_source` (youtube, linkedin, bio, embed, short_link).
