Show notes

Have you ever felt like a casual discussion with your partner suddenly turned into a full-scale boxing match? In this episode, we break down a fascinating consultation with retired referee Dr. Dan Gardner, who applies the sweet science of boxing to the art of marital communication.

Discover why marital left hooks target the ego rather than the jaw, how counter-punching leaves your midsection wide open, and why stepping forward into the conflict with the "analytic clinch" might just save your relationship. Whether you're looking to defuse high-stakes arguments or just figure out why your spouse always seems to land that second punch, this episode offers a humorous, insightful look at staying out of the referee's penalty box.

Tune in for expert psychological insights, relationship survival tips, and a fresh perspective on how to handle verbal sparring in your own home!
