Show notes

Neglecting walker ball maintenance is a fast track to "orthopedic pessimism" and a total collapse of your personal "Swagger Index". Without proper lubrication, those once-quiet tennis balls transform into screeching hallway menaces that "grab, stick, and screech" across the floor, effectively ending any hopes of "stealth grandparenting".

Instead of a smooth glide, you are left struggling with "sticky feet" that increase the physical effort required to move, leading to unnecessary strain on your shoulders and wrists. This failure to maintain your equipment doesn't just result in "audible friction" that ruins your dignity; it creates a safety hazard where your walker might suddenly catch on a sticky spot, causing the kind of jerks and stumbles that lead to "existential despair in clinical hallways". Essentially, dry balls are a "no-brainer" for anyone looking to replace effortless mobility with a loud, exhausting, and uncoordinated shuffle.
