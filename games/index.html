Games

Quick games built from the show’s real medicine. Come back tomorrow; the answers change and the co-pay doesn’t.

  • Daily Name that eponym Who’s behind McBurney’s point, the Capgras delusion or the Kocher maneuver? Five guesses, one new clue after each miss, a streak to protect.
  • Daily Word Rounds A medical word search on a new theme every day, from the biliary tract to Freud’s couch. Beat the clock, keep the streak.
  • Daily Line of the day One line a day from the episodes, with a card to share and a link to hear it in context.
