What these numbers mean, and where they come from

Median time in the ER: the typical time from arriving at the emergency department to leaving it, for patients who were sent home (CMS measure OP-18b), Oct 2024–Sep 2025. Half of visits took longer. Patients transferred elsewhere are excluded.

Mental health patients: the same median for psychiatric and mental health patients (OP-18c), who often wait far longer for a bed or transfer.

Left before being seen: the percentage of patients who left the ER before a clinician saw them (OP-22), Jan 2024–Dec 2024. Volume bands (low to very high) are CMS's own categories.

Source: CMS Care Compare, Timely and Effective Care, the hospital data behind Medicare's Care Compare. CMS last updated it 2026-07-22; fetched 2026-09-28. CMS refreshes it about quarterly, and hospitals with too few cases don't report a number.
